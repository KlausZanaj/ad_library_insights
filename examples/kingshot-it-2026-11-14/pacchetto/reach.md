# Reach and audience

Reach is published by Meta only for ads delivered in the EU or the UK. Locations are what the advertiser targeted and can name areas outside those regions.

Per day = reach in IT divided by the days the ad has been on air, seven at the least. Reach counts unique people: over months it grows slower than days, so compare totals too. When a concept runs as several ads, up to three of them are read and added up, each over its own days; EU and UK figures are those of its most shown ad. Days are those of the concept, with the age of its most shown ad in brackets when it differs.
