- Rating, 0-100, three parts that always weigh the same: scale, up to 60 points (people reached per day in IT; where that is not published, the position by impressions); staying power, up to 25 (days the concept has been running, full at 90); creative investment, up to 15 (different creatives for one concept, full at 10).
- Scale, where reach was read: relative to the best ad of this search, on a log scale. 100 for the one that reaches the most people per day among the ads of 7 days or more, 0 for one that reaches 1,000 times fewer.
- Scale of an ad that was not read, in a search where others were: a prudent estimate from its position in Meta's list, never above the weakest ad that was read. Not reading an ad never helps it.
- Scale where no reach is published: the position in Meta's whole list, on a log scale, 100 for the first.
- Staying power and creatives are measured on fixed thresholds, the same in every search.
- Reach per day = people reached in IT, divided by the days the ad has been on air (7 at the least, no upper limit). The EU and UK totals are information and do not rate.
- Reach counts unique people, who grow slower than days: an ad on air for months shows fewer people per day than it had at the start. Read the total reached next to the daily figure.
- Reliability of a rating: high = reach in the country searched was read; medium = not read, scale taken from the position in Meta's list; low = neither.
- Verdicts: scaling (14 to 89 days on air, scale of 70 or more and in the top third by people per day), holding (90 days or more with scale in the middle or above), testing (under 30 days; under 7 days an ad is too recent to judge), review (90 days or more, no ad added to the concept in that time, low scale and fewer people reached in all than the middle ad of the search: a small budget that works or a forgotten ad, to be checked, not a negative verdict), ended (no longer on air), average (the rest).
- Scaling and review compare ads with each other: they are given only on reach that was read in IT, and only when at least 5 concepts were read. Without that, no ad here is called scaling or review, whatever its position.
- One ad per concept: identical copies, versions of a dynamic ad, catalogue ads and translations are merged and do not count as creatives.
- Reach in IT: read for 12 of these 14 concepts. Meta publishes reach only for ads delivered in the EU or the UK, per ad. A concept that runs as several ads is listed once: up to 3 of its ads are read, the most shown ones, and their reach is added up; next to every figure is how many ads of how many were read. Reach is never added across concepts.
- Days: staying power counts from the oldest ad of the concept. When its most shown ad has a different age, both are written, because the reach belongs to the ads that were read.
- Meta publishes no spend and no sales. Scale and staying power are clues that an ad pays, not proof.
- The list on Meta is ordered by total impressions, highest first.

## Ad types
Every ad in the copy and creative files is to be sorted into one of these. The files give a hint from the format; the type itself comes from the text and from the pictures.
- catalogue or dynamic: a product feed or automatic versions fill the ad; nobody made this exact creative by hand
- UGC or creator: a customer or a creator films the product or talks to the camera in their own setting
- founder talking: someone from the company speaks in the first person about why the product exists
- product demo: the product in use: what it does, how, and the result
- gameplay: footage of the game or the app itself, real or staged
- static promo: one picture carrying an offer, a price or a claim
- product carousel: several cards, one product or one argument per card
- other: none of the above; say what it is in three words
